How it works

A seat is a dollar. The door mints one seat for one dollar while the machine has room. Sell any seat back for a dollar from the tank, any time. No fee either way.

It snows. The bot trades the reserve. Every round trip it banks, half goes to seat holders the same minute. Your $RAINBOWSNOW balance just rises.

New seats warm up. Founding seats are warm from the start. After the founding phase, a new seat earns nothing for three days, then turns warm on its own; its share meanwhile goes to the seats already here. Nothing is ever locked.

The rainbow gun never sells. Ten cents of every profit dollar buy and hold the family coins, burn $GAWLAK, and pay for surprises. Thirty cents keep the tank full and compound the rest into the machine. Capacity is measured, never promised.

The rainbow

Every profit dollar: 50¢ snows on seats · 30¢ fills the tank, then compounds into the machine (Rainbow Rain on every seat once both are full) · 10¢ runs operations, builder included · 10¢ is the rainbow gun, which buys, holds, burns and surprises, and never sells.
